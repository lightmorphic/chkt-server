Where CHKT sends you problem reports and test messages. Standard SMTP, any provider.

Local backups

A dated JSON snapshot is kept in the server's data folder every day. Use "Download everything" below any time you want a copy on your own hard drive.

Quiet hours

A do-not-disturb schedule for CHKT itself. Reminders still fire and appear during quiet hours, they just don't ring, speak, or vibrate.

"From" can be later than "Until", that means overnight (e.g. 22:00 to 07:00).

Calendar (CalDAV)

Subscribe to your reminders from any calendar app — DAVx⁵ on Android, Thunderbird, Evolution or Apple Calendar on a desktop. Anything you add to the CHKT calendar becomes a reminder: notification and voice, repeating every 5 minutes for an hour until you answer it.

Username: anything you like. Password: a device access key from Devices — the same keys the phone app uses. Use it over HTTPS: the key travels on every request.

An all-day event has no time of day, so a reminder made from one needs to be given a time.
