Sound first, then the words

The two never talk over each other. Pick per reminder:

  • BOTHA sound, then your reminder spoken aloud
  • VOICEJust the voice, no ding in front of it
  • NOTIFYA sound and a banner, nothing spoken

It doesn't give up after one go

Re-alert every 1, 2 or 5 minutes until you press Done, with an automatic stop from 15 minutes to 2 hours so it never nags into the night. Snooze lengths are chosen when the alert fires, not when you set it.

On your calendar, both ways

A CalDAV calendar any app can subscribe to. Tag a reminder and it appears. Add an event there and it becomes a reminder.

Every rhythm you actually use

Daily, chosen weekdays, monthly by date or last day, yearly, or any interval down to the minute.

Questions people ask before installing

Do I need to run the server?

No. The Android app works on its own, completely offline, with no account and no server. The server adds a browser version, a calendar other apps can subscribe to, and sync between the two. Install the app first and add the server later if you want those.

Does CHKT work without Google Play Services?

Yes. There is no Firebase, no Play Services dependency and no Google account anywhere in it. Alarms use Android's own exact alarm scheduling, location reminders use the platform's proximity services, and speech uses whichever engine you install. It runs on de-Googled phones.

What does CHKT cost?

Nothing. The app and the server are both free and licensed GPL-3.0, so you can read the source, change it, or run it unmodified. There is no paid tier, no subscription and no in-app purchase. Hosting costs whatever your own machine costs to run.

Which voice reads the reminders out?

Whichever text-to-speech engine is installed on your phone, because CHKT bundles no voice of its own. If it finds no engine at all, it points you at SherpaTTS, a free offline engine on F-Droid.

Will it actually wake me up?

CHKT schedules alerts the way an alarm clock does, so they fire on time through Doze and battery saver rather than waiting for the phone to wake. The alert shows full screen over the lock screen, survives a reboot, and repeats until you answer it.

Can I see reminders in my normal calendar app?

Yes, once you run the server. It publishes a CalDAV calendar that DAVx⁵, Thunderbird, Evolution, Apple Calendar and Fastmail can subscribe to. Tag a reminder and it appears there. Add an event to that calendar and it becomes a reminder.
