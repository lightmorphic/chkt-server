Reminders that talk, wherever you are.

CHKT Server is the self-hosted browser companion to CHKT, the talking reminders app for Android. Run it on your own hardware and every feature of the phone app is there in your browser too, kept in step by two-way sync.

Everything the app does. Same reminders, same voice.

Sync is optional and off by default on the phone. Turn it on and both sides stay matched, edit a reminder anywhere and it catches up everywhere else.

Everything coming up, in order

The home screen shows every reminder in time order with tag chips to filter, no folders to dig through.

Flexible repeats

Daily, weekly on chosen days, monthly (day or last day), yearly, or any custom interval down to the minute.

Ring, then speak

Four alert styles per reminder, ring and speak, ring only, speak only, or a plain notification, with an optional tone before the speech.

Keeps reminding you

Set it to re-alert every 1, 2, or 5 minutes until you answer, with an automatic stop from 15 minutes to 2 hours so it never nags forever.

Quiet hours

A do-not-disturb schedule for CHKT itself. Reminders still fire and appear, they just don't ring, speak, or vibrate during the window you set.

Snooze, decided in the moment

The snooze length is chosen when the alert happens, from 10 minutes to a day, never locked in ahead of time.

Add a reminder by voice

Say "remind me at 2pm to feed the cat" and the form fills itself in. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere until you save.

Location reminders

Trigger when the phone arrives at or leaves a place. Set the location from the app, or from your browser with one click.

Install it like an app

A proper PWA, add it to your home screen or dock, with desktop push notifications so it can reach you even when the tab is closed.

Backups

Daily snapshots kept locally, plus a one-click zip download any time you want a copy on your own hard drive.

Plain-file export

Export everything as JSON (round-trips back in) or Markdown to read. Your data is yours, in a format anyone can open.

Two-factor sign-in

Optional TOTP with any authenticator app, on top of a scrypt-hashed password and hardened session cookies.

Per-device access keys

Every phone gets its own sync key from the Devices page, revoke one any time without touching the others.

Tells you when there's an update

A quiet notice on the Settings page when a newer release is out on GitHub, checked at most once a day.

Completion stats

A 30-day view of what got done, missed, or snoozed, and your completion rate.

One command to install

Requires Docker with the compose plugin. No configuration file needed to try it, CHKT generates and keeps its own secret key.

git clone https://github.com/lightmorphic/chkt-server
cd chkt-server
docker compose up -d --build

Open http://<this-machine>:8321 and the first visit creates your account.

On your own network

The command above is enough for a home network or a private network like Tailscale. Everything, email alerts, backups, quiet hours, is configured afterwards on the Settings page.

On the public internet

Add the bundled Caddy setup for a real domain with automatic HTTPS, no certificates to manage by hand. Full steps are in the README.

Your server, your data

Everything lives in your own SQLite database on your own machine. No account with us, nothing phones out except what you explicitly configure (email).

Encrypted at rest

Every credential you enter is encrypted with a key derived from your server's secret key. A stolen copy of the database alone gives up nothing.
